SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS
SPECIFICATION (SRS) – PHASE 1
Lotus Funds – Research & Broker Distribution Platform
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
This document defines the software requirements, system actors, role hierarchy, permissions,
workflows, and operational boundaries for the Lotus Funds Research & Broker Distribution
Platform.
The platform is designed for live production usage in the financial research domain,
ensuring:
● Regulatory separation between research and non-research content
● Independence of SEBI-registered Research Analysts (RAs)
● Broker-neutral research distribution
● Compliance-safe white-label content delivery
● Clear liability demarcation
1.2 Scope
The Lotus Funds platform enables:
● Distribution of SEBI-registered Research Analyst research calls
● Publishing of RA-authored articles and content with mandatory disclaimers
● Broker-facilitated delivery of research and content to end clients

===== PAGE 2 =====
● White-label distribution of Lotus Funds–created non-research content
● Subscription-based access for platform users
Explicit Exclusions
● The platform does not execute trades
● The platform does not guarantee returns
● The platform does not approve, edit, delay, or suppress research calls
● The platform does not assume research liability for third-party RAs

2. SYSTEM ACTORS & ROLE MODEL
Core Principle
Research Analysts are regulated, independent system actors.
Research generation and non-research content creation are explicitly separated.
Actor Overview
● Super Admin (Platform Governance)
● Admin (Broker Entity)
● Research Analyst (SEBI-Registered)
○ External RA (Independent / Broker-Hired)
○ Lotus Funds RA (Organizational)
● Content & Design Roles (Non-Regulated)
● End Users (Platform Client, Broker-Only Client, Guest User)

4. RESEARCH ANALYST (SEBI-REGISTERED ENTITY)
4.1 Research Analyst – Core Definition
A Research Analyst (RA) is a SEBI-registered regulated entity authorized to publish research
calls and research-related content.
Common Rules
● Must be SEBI-registered and verified
● Cannot operate anonymously
● Mandatory disclaimer required on all research and articles
● Responsible for accuracy, logic, and compliance of research
● Cannot manage platform users or system settings
4.2 External Research Analyst
Includes:
● Independent RAs
● Broker-Hired RAs
Permissions
● Generate and publish research calls
● Publish their own articles, blogs, and market commentary
● Distribute research:
○ Directly to platform clients
○ Via subscribed brokers
Liability

===== PAGE 7 =====
● Full liability for research and content rests with the RA or Broker
● Lotus Funds provides distribution infrastructure only
● Lotus Funds holds no liability for RA-authored research or articles
4.3 Lotus Funds Research Analyst (Organizational RA)
● SEBI-registered RAs operating under Lotus Funds
Permissions
● Publish research calls under Lotus Funds RA identity
● Distribute research directly or via subscribed brokers
Liability
● Research liability lies with Lotus Funds as an RA entity

6. RESEARCH CALL LIFECYCLE & DISTRIBUTION
6.1 Call Creation
● Only SEBI-registered RAs can create research calls
● Mandatory fields:
○ Instrument / Symbol
○ Buy / Sell
○ Entry, Target, Stop-Loss, Lot Size
○ Time horizon
○ Automatically calculated Risk-Reward ratio
○ Mandatory disclaimer
6.2 Call Failure & Rejection Handling
A research call shall be automatically rejected if:
● A network or server issue prevents message registration
● RA or Broker subscription has expired or payment is overdue
Rejected calls
● Are not published
● Are not visible to any user
● Do not enter the published research audit trail
● Trigger a notification to the originating RA

===== PAGE 11 =====
6.3 Call Immutability & Errata Mechanism
● Published research calls are immutable and cannot be edited or deleted
● If an RA identifies an error in published values, the RA may issue an Errata
Errata Rules
● Errata can be generated only by the originating RA
● Errata references the original call
● Original and corrected messages are permanently stored
● All users who received the original call are notified
● Errata is immutable once published
Performance Calculation
● Performance sheets ignore the original call
● Profit / loss is calculated only using Errata values
6.4 Call Targeting
At creation, RA must explicitly select the audience:
● One specific broker
● Multiple selected brokers
● All subscribers of that RA
6.5 Broker Visibility Rules
Broker CAN:

===== PAGE 12 =====
● View calls sent to their clients
● View RA performance metrics
Broker CANNOT:
● Edit or approve calls
● Delay or suppress calls
● Modify disclaimers or research logic
6.6 White-Labelling Rules
Allowed
● Broker logo
● Broker contact details
Not Allowed
● Editing research text
● Editing entry / target / SL
● Editing timestamps
● Editing disclaimers
● Removing RA attribution

II. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Authentication & Authorization
The platform shall implement a secure authentication and authorization framework based
on Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
Authentication Capabilities
● Secure user login using:
○ Username and password
○ OTP-based verification where applicable
● Session management including:
○ Automatic session timeout after inactivity
○ Activity-based logout for security enforcement
Authorization Model
● Hierarchical role enforcement across the platform:
○ Super Admin (Platform Governance)
○ Admin (Broker Entity)
○ Research Analyst (SEBI-Registered)
○ Content & Design Roles (Non-Regulated)
○ End Users (Platform Client / Broker-Only Client / Guest User)
Authorization Rules
● Every system request shall undergo role and permission validation.

===== PAGE 21 =====
● Users shall only access features explicitly permitted to their assigned role.
● Permissions related to research and non-research content shall be strictly segregated.
● Unauthorized access attempts shall be logged for audit and security review.
3.2 Content Management (Non-Research Content)
The platform shall provide a content management system exclusively for non-research
content.
Authorized Roles
● Content Admin
● Admin (Broker Entity)
● Super Admin
● Lotus Funds Internal Content & Design Team
Supported Content Types
● Educational articles
● Market updates (non-call content)
● Blogs
● Newsletters
● White-label informational banners
Functional Behavior
● Authorized users may create, edit, publish, archive, or unpublish non-research content.
● All content must be explicitly classified as non-research at the time of creation.

===== PAGE 22 =====
● Mandatory attribution (Author and/or Organization) shall be displayed on all content.
● Version history and edit logs shall be maintained for all content items.
Restrictions
● Content Admins and design teams cannot create or publish research calls.
● Non-research content shall not contain explicit buy/sell/hold recommendations or price
levels.
3.3 Research Call Management
The platform shall support the creation, distribution, and tracking of research calls authored
exclusively by SEBI-registered Research Analysts (RAs).
Authorized Roles
● Research Analyst (SEBI-Registered)
● Super Admin (only if registered as a Research Analyst)
Mandatory Research Call Fields
Each research call shall include:
● Instrument / Symbol name
● Action (Buy / Sell / Hold)
● Entry price
● Target price
● Stop-loss
● Time horizon

===== PAGE 23 =====
● Mandatory risk disclaimer
● Research ownership classification:
○ Independent RA
○ Broker-Hired RA
○ Organizational RA (Lotus Funds)
Research Call Lifecycle
● Research calls shall follow a controlled lifecycle:
○ Draft → Publish
● Once published:
○ Research calls become immutable
○ Editing or deletion is not permitted
Errata Mechanism (Correction Handling)
● In case of factual or input error, the originating RA may issue an Errata:
○ Errata references the original call
○ Original and corrected calls are permanently preserved
○ Errata is immutable once published
○ All recipients of the original call are notified
● Performance calculations shall ignore the original call and consider only the Errata
values.
Audit & Traceability

===== PAGE 24 =====
● All research call actions (creation, publication, errata issuance) shall be recorded in an
immutable audit trail.
Distribution Rules
● At the time of creation, the RA must explicitly select the target audience:
○ One or more specific brokers
○ Direct platform subscribers
○ All subscribers of the RA
● Brokers shall not be able to edit, approve, delay, suppress, or modify research calls.
3.4 Broker Management
The platform shall support broker onboarding, approval, and lifecycle management.
Functional Capabilities
● Broker onboarding request submission
● Broker type selection:
○ API Broker (future scope)
○ Non-API Broker (current scope)
● Document submission and compliance verification
● Broker approval, suspension, or reactivation
Controls
● Broker access shall be enabled only after Super Admin approval.
● Suspended brokers shall lose access to:

===== PAGE 25 =====
○ Research distribution
○ Subscription management
● All broker activities shall be logged for audit purposes.
3.5 Research Analyst Management
The platform shall support Research Analyst onboarding, verification, and publishing
control.
Functional Capabilities
● RA onboarding with SEBI registration verification
● RA classification:
○ Independent Research Analyst
○ Broker-Hired Research Analyst
○ Organizational Research Analyst (Lotus Funds)
● Assignment to broker(s) where applicable
● Publishing scope enforcement based on RA classification
Enforcement Rules
● Only verified and active RAs may publish research calls.
● Broker-hired RAs are restricted to their assigned broker scope.
● RA publishing rights shall be automatically suspended upon SEBI registration expiry or
account suspension.

3.6 Subscription Management
The platform shall manage subscription-based access to research and non-research content.
Subscription Types
● Platform client subscriptions
● Broker-specific subscriptions
● Research Analyst-specific subscriptions
Functional Rules
● Subscription start and expiry dates shall be strictly enforced.
● Access to content and research shall be automatically revoked upon subscription expiry.
● Users shall receive automated reminders prior to subscription expiration.
● Payment gateway integration shall support:
○ Subscription purchase
○ Renewal
○ Cancellation (where applicable)
3.7 Notifications
The platform shall provide a centralized notification system.
Notification Types
● Research call alerts and Errata notifications
● Content publication updates

===== PAGE 27 =====
● Subscription expiry reminders
● Platform and broker announcements
Delivery Channels
● In-platform notifications
● Email notifications
● Messaging channels where integrated
